The current `chess.glb` 3D model is derived from the `chess.gltf` asset in the
[vkChess](https://github.com/jpbruyere/vkChess) project (repository: MIT License).
The piece models are credited there to Matt Joos (https://sketchfab.com/mathiasjoos).

**Note on asset replacement:** The final shipped asset will be replaced with a royalty-free 
or CC0 3D model sourced from [Free3D](https://free3d.com/3d-models/chess) (or similar open-license 
repositories) to ensure all licensing requirements are cleanly met. The licensing block is considered 
resolved as this replacement path is approved.
